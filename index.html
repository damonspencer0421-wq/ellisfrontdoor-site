Reading the report

Work done, activity, and results are three different things

Other companies blur these together. Your report keeps them apart.

1 · DeliveredWhat I did

Every post, update, request and reply, with dates. This is what I guarantee.

2 · ActivityWhat people did

Profile views, website clicks, direction requests, taps on your call button. Google counts a tap on "Call" as a call, even if nobody talked. I label it as a tap.

3 · ResultsWhat you booked

Real calls, estimates and jobs. Only you know these, so we track them together. A rise doesn't automatically mean I caused it, and I won't claim it did.

Nobody can honestly promise that, and every angry review above came from a company that did.

What I promise is everything listed on your plan, every month. The posts, the Google updates, a review request to every customer you send me, a written reply to every review, and your details submitted to the major directories.

Straight answers

What owners ask first

Why pay you instead of using Canva or ChatGPT myself?

You can. Most owners start, then stop by week three because the business comes first. What you're paying for is it getting done every week, checked for wrong prices and wrong details, answered when reviews come in, and written down so you can see it happened.

Have you done this for a business like mine?

Not yet for a paying client. I'm taking my first ones now. What I can show you is the system I run every day on my own pages, the demo work above, and a Reality Check on your own business before you commit to anything monthly.

Will this get me more calls?

It should help, and I'll show you the numbers that suggest whether it is. I won't promise calls, because nobody honestly can. See "Reading the report" above for what I measure and how I keep work done separate from results.

How much work is this for me?

About an hour to set up: the intake form and adding me as a manager on your accounts. After that the goal is under 30 minutes a month: approving posts and sending me customer contacts so they get review requests.

What if I don't like a post?

You see every month's posts 5 days early. Flag anything within 48 hours and I'll change it. Two rounds of changes a month are included. If you don't reply, posts go out as scheduled, and you can pause any post at any time. Review replies are different: they never post without your OK.

Who owns my Google, Facebook and Instagram?

You do, always. I'm added as a manager and never become the owner. When you leave, you remove me or I remove myself, same day. Google Business Profile is free from Google. My fee is for the work of managing it, not for the profile.

What happens if I cancel?

Tell me any time before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. No fee, no notice period. Within 5 business days you get every file I made for you, and my access is removed.

Are there any other costs?

No. The software I use is included. The only extra would be ad spend, and only if you choose to run ads. That money goes straight to the ad platform from your card.

How do review requests reach my customers?

By email, or by text only for customers who've agreed to get texts from you. It depends on how you book jobs and take payment, and we set it up together in week one. Every customer you send me gets asked, not only the happy ones.
